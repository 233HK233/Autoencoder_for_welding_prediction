Figure 3 package

This folder collects the artifacts used to generate the final Figure 3 export.

Contents

- `figure_exports/`
  Final Figure 3 outputs from `outputs/paper_figures/fig3_h1_comparison_layout_v03/`:
  - `figure3_h1_comparison.pdf`
  - `figure3_h1_comparison.svg`
  - `figure3_h1_comparison.png`
  - `figure3_h1_comparison_panel_values.csv`

- `source_tables/`
  CSV tables consumed by `render_figure3_matplotlib.py`:
  - `exploratory_ordered_run_registry.csv`
  - `exploratory_ordered_class_metrics_summary.csv`
  - `comparison_summary.csv`

- `parsed_metric_files/`
  Per-method `evaluation_metrics.txt` files parsed to extract test accuracy,
  macro-F1, precision, recall, and F1 values:
  - `Teacher/evaluation_metrics.txt`
  - `Student/evaluation_metrics.txt`
  - `GRU/evaluation_metrics.txt`
  - `LSTM/evaluation_metrics.txt`
  - `Inception/evaluation_metrics.txt`
  - `Transformer/evaluation_metrics.txt`
